隱私權政策

  1. 本應用程式只由擁有者本人使用。
  2. 它透過 Google Drive API 存取擁有者自己的 Google 雲端硬碟檔案，用途只有上傳、下載與整理檔案。
  3. 檔案與授權資料只存在擁有者自己的電腦與自己的 Google 帳號裡，不會傳送、分享或販售給任何第三方。
  4. 不蒐集其他人的個人資料，不使用追蹤工具或 cookie。
  5. 要撤銷授權：到 Google 帳戶的「第三方應用程式與服務」移除 rclone。

Privacy Policy

  1. This application is used only by its owner.
  2. It accesses the owner's own Google Drive files through the Google Drive API, solely to upload, download and organise files.
  3. Files and authorisation data stay on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own Google account. They are not sent to, shared with or sold to any third party.
  4. It does not collect anyone else's personal data and does not use tracking tools or cookies.
  5. To revoke access, remove rclone under "Third-party apps & services" in the Google Account settings.
